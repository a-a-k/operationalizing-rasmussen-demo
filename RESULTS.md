# Results and interpretation

## Evolving state, movement and constraints

Eight runner histories produced 181 report batches, each with two logical-group
assessments. Reports within a history are dependent. [Complete evidence](evolving-state/results/campaign-v0.1.1/README.md)
retains events, component correspondence, policies, availability and diagnoses.

| Observer | Available | Correct state/policy/margin | Wrong policy decisions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Fixed initial schema | 129 | 80 | 48 |
| Updated direct fractions | 304 | 304 | 0 |
| Updated complete relative state | 304 | 304 | 0 |

The gain over the fixed schema concerns maintaining correspondence and policy
interpretation. Updated fractions matched the complete-state observer. The
histories recorded 667 externally successful calls whose third attempt succeeded
after two unsuccessful attempts under controlled delivery faults.

The [directional readout](evolving-state/results/campaign-v0.1.1/directional-v0.1.0/README.md)
uses saved causal prefixes. Direction and policies were available in 119/181
report batches. All 238 latest/smoothed conditional diagnoses agreed with
independent continuation in the original parts. The [reduced log-odds replay](evolving-state/results/campaign-v0.1.1/directional-v0.1.0/reduced-v0.1.0/README.md)
also reproduced every orientation and first constraint on this mask; these
outputs do not require the full composition.

At equal catalog counts and a 50% limit, one transition moved away and another
toward the limit. Revising both limits from 50% to 20% at unchanged state and
direction reduced conditional first-contact distance from 1.393814 to 0.193248.
Thirteen policy-only reassessments preserved measured state and direction;
five changed current compliance.

The supported scenario establishes these implemented measurement behaviors.
Retry limits have not been validated as physical failure boundaries; conditional
continuations have not been validated as future execution forecasts or beneficial
interventions.

## CPU representation and MEWMA

The [CPU study](drift-monitor/results/campaign-v0.1.1/README.md) uses eleven
consumption parts and ten orthonormal contrasts for each representation.
Full covariance, temporal weighting, telemetry and background procedure are
shared. The frozen model is reproduced from eight fitting and twelve calibration
histories.

The selection comprises 250 first-qualified pairs from 273 attempts. All attempts
and qualification reasons remain available. Both original observers detected
all 250 reallocation histories. ILR was earlier in 207 pairs, tied in 40, and
later in three.

| ILR threshold | Mean advance favoring ILR, cycles | Control signals, ILR / raw |
|---|---:|---:|
| Original `h` | 1.252 | 9 / 6 |
| Retrospective `1.10h` | 0.972 | 6 / 6 |

Each cycle lasts 40 seconds; delay is restricted to twelve post-intervention
cycles. The original advance is 50.08 seconds in restricted mean delay.
The stricter threshold reuses evaluation controls retrospectively. Equal observed
control counts do not establish equal population false-alarm rates. Control
signals without planned transfers are not automatically false positives.

The result supports representation-dependent detection in this setting.
Representation, covariance estimation and calibration were not causally
separated. Service-violation lead time and intervention benefits were not measured.
